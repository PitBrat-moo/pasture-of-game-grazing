CYBER‑JANK PASTURE
==================
Industrial, broken, atmospheric, and mechanically honest titles — finished or not.

CORE AE / CYBER‑JANK
--------------------
• CyberMage: Darklight Awakening (1995) — proto‑cyber‑RPG shooter, D.W. Bradley design, pure origin of AE tone  
• Restricted Area (2004) — cyber‑RPG, industrial solitude, AE cornerstone  
• Metalheart: Replicants Rampage (2005) — desert cyber‑wasteland, broken but beautiful  
• Space Hack (2004) — endless procedural grind, eerie isolation  
• GeneTroopers (2006) — Euro‑cyberpunk shooter‑RPG hybrid, dense atmosphere  
• Alpha Prime (2007) — Czech industrial shooter, claustrophobic tone  
• Liquidator 2: Welcome to Hell (2008) — surreal Russian sci‑fi FPS, heavy lighting  
• E.Y.E: Divine Cybermancy (2011) — surreal systems, philosophical depth  
• Planet Alcatraz (2009) — prison‑planet RPG, grim humor  
• Insomnia: The Ark (2018) — dieselpunk dystopia, broken brilliance  
• Encased (2021) — corporate wasteland CRPG, modern AE benchmark  
• UnderRail (2015) — subterranean isolation, pure AE pacing  
• ATOM RPG / Trudograd (2018–2021) — Soviet post‑collapse tone, deliberate pacing

• Space Siege (2008) — sterile but salvageable under AE

MODERN SPIRITUALS
-----------------
• The Fall (2014) — isolation, machinery, moral decay

NOTES
-----
These titles embody the **industrial melancholy and mechanical honesty** of the AE doctrine — broken, ambitious, and immersive through imperfection.
