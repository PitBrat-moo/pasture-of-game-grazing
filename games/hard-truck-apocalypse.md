## Notes

- Community Patch recommended.
- Community Remaster includes extensive graphics and gameplay changes.
- Steam players must temporarily switch language to Russian before installing Community Patch / Remaster.
- Use RTSS for a strict 60 FPS cap.
- Lossless Scaling works well from a 60 FPS base framerate.
- Fast travel and quest scripting remain stable using the Community Patch.

⚠️ Stable under Windows 10/11 with RTSS 60 FPS cap
Avoid uncapped framerates.
