## Notes

- Official Patch 1.3 recommended.
- NOCD executable required for reliable operation on modern systems.
- Delete or rename intro.avi to prevent startup hangs.
- dgVoodoo is required for stable DirectX rendering.
- Use SRWE to control window size and position.
- Game supports very high framerates on modern hardware.
- Driver-level anisotropic filtering produces the best image quality.

⚠️ Stable under Windows 10/11 after dgVoodoo installation and intro movie removal

Startup hangs are typically caused by the intro video and are resolved through the fixes described in the installation guide.
